<objective>
Reposition the persistent sidebar-toggle chevron button in `src/ui/AppShell.tsx` so that on desktop (non-touch) with the sidebar OPEN it rides the sidebar's inner (right) edge instead of always sitting at viewport-left. Touch behavior and the sidebar-CLOSED desktop position stay exactly as they are today. The chevron must slide in sync with the sidebar's own `width` transition, and skip the transition during a drag-resize (mirroring the sidebar column at line 1554).

Purpose: The chevron currently overlaps the top of the open sidebar's content at viewport-left, which is visually awkward now that the AppRail is retired. Moving it to the sidebar's inner edge makes it read as "the sidebar's own close handle" when open while preserving its "open the sidebar" affordance when closed.

Output: One-file edit to `src/ui/AppShell.tsx` (style object on the chevron `<button>` at lines ~1518-1522), plus verification via `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx vitest run src/ui/AppShell.persistence.test.tsx`, plus one atomic commit.
</objective>
