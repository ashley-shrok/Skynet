<objective>
Fix the WIP-indicator drop diagnosed on Tina 2026-09-02: SSH `Channel open failure` on the `cat /proc/<pid>/stat` exec is being conflated with real PID-dead ENOENT because the channel adapter swallows the transport error to `null`, and `isStaleFromStat(_, null)` returns `true` (stale). The reaper then publishes `session_gone` and the frontend row drops, killing the WIP indicator on a session that is actively working.

Fix strategy (per fix-wip-indicator-transport-vs-dead.md — LOCKED):
Introduce a `readStatWithSentinel(channel, pid)` helper in `ssh-poll-orchestrator.ts` that wraps the stat exec with an exit-code sentinel (`cat /proc/${pid}/stat 2>&1 && echo __STAT_OK__ || echo __STAT_ENOENT__`) and returns a tagged discriminated union distinguishing three outcomes: `{ok: true, content}`, `{ok: false, reason: 'enoent'}`, `{ok: false, reason: 'transport'}`. Update the two reap call sites (processPid ~L1119/L1565 and sweepOneHost ~L1959) to skip the reap on `transport` (fail-open — keep the session in livenessMap this tick) while preserving today's reap behavior on `enoent` and on ok-with-mismatched-field22.

The pure `liveness-check.ts` contract stays unchanged. The `starter.ts` channel adapter stays unchanged (other consumers keep the null-on-error semantic). All changes are localized to `ssh-poll-orchestrator.ts` and its test file.

Purpose: Stop reaping live sessions on transient sshd channel-open-failure. Restore Tina's WIP indicator (and any other session whose stat exec loses the sshd MaxSessions coin-flip).
Output: One helper + two call-site updates + four regression tests, all shippable in a single commit.
</objective>
