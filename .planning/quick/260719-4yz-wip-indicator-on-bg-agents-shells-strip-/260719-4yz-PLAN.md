<objective>
Patch #72 — Two coupled tweaks to the pretty-view work-in-progress indicator, driven by semantics:

1. **Broaden the trigger.** WipBubble mounts when EITHER Claude is in an active turn (`wipActive`) OR there are backgrounded agents/shells running. From Ashley's perspective, all three states mean the same thing: "session is busy, come back later." The current condition (`wipActive` alone) under-reports busy-ness whenever Claude has already handed control back but subagents or `run_in_background:true` bash shells are still churning.

2. **Strip the glass-card bubble from WipBubble.** The current bubble makes WIP visually indistinguishable from an assistant message for the first ~200ms — the eye parses "bubble" as "message." Stripping the card and floating a slightly larger naked spinner makes "spinner alone" instantly readable as an indicator, not content. PlanPendingBubble intentionally keeps its bubble because plan-pending IS "idle, waiting on you" — same semantics as a normal message, so message-shaped chrome is correct there.

Purpose: Reliable at-a-glance parsing of session state — busy (spinner, no bubble) vs. message (bubble) vs. waiting-on-you (plan-pending bubble).
Output: Two-file diff on PrettyView.tsx and WipBubble.tsx, plus a housekeeping step to close the bounty folder.
</objective>
