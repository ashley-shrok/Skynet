<objective>
Move the bounty-bounty intent transform from ComposeBox's four send-path call sites into
useVoiceRecording's endAppend/endSend, so the transformation happens at STT-emit time
(when the voice-derived text is glued into the textarea) rather than at send-dispatch
time. This restores the "voice feels native" UX (users see "/bounty …" in the textarea
immediately after speaking) and cleanly removes the accidental typed-text transform.

Purpose: Per Ashley — the "bounty bounty" affordance is a voice-only mnemonic because
you can't easily speak a slash. It was never intended to fire on typed text, and it
should never leave the raw "bounty bounty …" phrase sitting in the textarea for the
voice-append user to see.

Output: A single quick patch that touches exactly 3 source files (useVoiceRecording.ts,
ComposeBox.tsx, useVoiceRecording.test.ts), passes typecheck + full vitest suite, and
leaves composeIntentTransform.ts (the pure function) and its unit tests unchanged.
</objective>
