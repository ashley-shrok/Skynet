<objective>
Add two independent, atomic instrumentation touch points to Skynet backend so the next PWA boot produces the data needed to attribute slow-auth latency to one of three suspects: (1) event-loop starvation before handlers run, (2) time spent inside the auth middleware / handler, or (3) time spent in the browser/edge queue before the request lands. Instrumentation-only — no functional behavior change to /users/* routes.

Purpose: SLICE 1 of bounty `auth-slow-requests-on-pwa-boot`. Without this instrumentation we cannot tell whether the observed slow-auth warnings originate at the process, the handler, or upstream — every attempt at a fix is a guess until this data lands.

Output: `src/backend/starter.ts` (event-loop lag sampler in boot IIFE, SIGTERM-safe, VITEST-gated), `src/backend/database/routes/users.ts` (router-level timing middleware).
