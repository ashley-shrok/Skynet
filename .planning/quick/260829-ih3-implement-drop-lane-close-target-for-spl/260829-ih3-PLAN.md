<objective>
Implement the collapsed-panel drop-lane close-target for split view per the frozen shape at `.planning/shapes/shape-drop-lane-close-in-split-view.md`.

**What:** A ~115px vertical lane that slides in from the left edge during an identity-badge drag when the conv-list panel is collapsed, standing in for the panel as a badge drop-to-close target. Neutral baseline (matches the app's chrome), coral-on-hover (matches every other drop target's hover semantic), single centered `X` glyph, closes the dragged tab on drop.

**Why:** Right now, closing a session from split view when the panel is collapsed requires reopening the panel first — a two-motion gesture. This surface makes it one motion, without dragging the panel open (which would compete with row-drop-to-open semantics).

**Load-bearing constraint (Ashley, 2026-08-29):** the app's coral vocabulary means "you are hovering a valid drop target NOW." A baseline-coral lane would mislead the eye into thinking the drag was already at its destination. The lane MUST render neutral at rest and MUST only turn coral on dragover. Same rule every other drop target in the app follows (SplitView.tsx:439-454, PrettyConversationsPanel :1294-1412, AppShell empty-PV :2477-2488).

Purpose: Ship the surface described in the shape file; do NOT alter the underlying close routine, do NOT touch PrettyConversationsPanel / IdentityBadge / SplitView, do NOT introduce a new close semantic — this is the existing close reachable from a new surface.

Output: One new component (with colocated hook), one new colocated regression test file, and a ~3-line wiring change in AppShell.tsx (import + mount + prop pass-through).
</objective>
