Modal tasting · pass two · comprehensive (25 modals · all categories)

Assistant Bubble style, applied to real Skynet modals.

Pass one settled the direction — hue-tinted body, warm cream text, head/foot separators. This page applies it to six actual modals from the app, spanning the categories that stretch the design hardest: small confirm · single-form · list-manager · multi-pane settings · required-shell reload.

If the pattern holds across these six, the remaining ~19 real modals follow by analogy and I'll build them in a batch. The identity hue varies per modal in this pass — warm gold for identity-scoped work, neutral blue-gray for app-level settings/feedback, warm amber for the reload-required "attention" case. Real modals will inherit whichever hue their context owns.

The stages behind each modal show mocked pretty-view bubbles peeking through the dim, same as pass one. React to modal-in-context, not modal-in-isolation.

Category · Multi-pane settings

Category · Required shell action

Reload — client out of date

The reload-required modal — fires when the client build mismatches the server. Non-dismissible (no close X, no cancel, backdrop click does nothing). Warm amber hue (40°) instead of an identity or app default — signals "attention needed" without alarming red, and visually distinguishes it as a system-level thing rather than a chat-scoped thing. Halo is a touch stronger too — the whole point of this modal is to be seen.

Questions to react to

  • Does the pattern hold across all six? The confirm, form, settings, list, and shell-required all use the same head-separator-body-separator-foot anatomy. Small confirms are compact; the settings modal is 640×480; the shell modal centers its lone button. Same recipe, different sizes.
  • Neutral hue for app-level modals (Preferences, general Feedback) — right call? Alternative: they inherit whatever identity you're currently talking to. Neutral says "this is the app, not an agent"; inherited says "even the app wears the identity's clothing."
  • The list-manager pattern — bubbles-inside-a-bubble. Rows are each their own hue-tinted mini-bubble, sitting inside the modal's own hue-tinted shell. Cool or too busy?
  • The reload modal's amber hue. Warm-warning amber (40°) says "attention needed" without red. Alternative: keep it hue-neutral like Preferences (identity-agnostic). Alternative: give it the red-destructive treatment (the version drift IS blocking).
  • Preferences nav-column treatment. Darker sub-surface with hue-tinted active row. Reads naturally, or too heavy against the hue-tinted body?
  • Anything a size-class needs I haven't shown — remaining categories are content-editors (Runbook, Skills, Identity, Role, EditableFile, ProjectFile) and the auth-prompt family (SSH auth, host-key, OPKSSH, Warpgate). I have designs in mind for both; want to see either category before I mock the rest?

Once you tell me the pattern holds (or where it needs adjustment), I'll build the remaining ~19 modals in a batch and hand you a comprehensive reference page.
