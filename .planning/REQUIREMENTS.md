# Requirements: Skynet

Requirements are recorded per phase, as D-numbered decisions in each phase's
`.planning/phases/<NN>-<slug>/<NN>-CONTEXT.md`, and per design effort in the
shape files under `.planning/shapes/`, `.planning/campaigns/`, and
`.planning/`. There is no project-wide REQ-ID list for current work.

The REQ-ID list used by Phases 1–19 (TOGGLE, RENDER, COMPOSE, BACKEND,
FALLBACK, CHANGEOVER, UPLOAD, TG, PURGE, ASIDE, PIN, RELAYBUB, IDMEDIT,
TTSSTR, and others) is archived at `.planning/milestones/v1.0-REQUIREMENTS.md`.

Shipped capabilities and open work are summarized in `.planning/PROJECT.md`.
