Wave-2 backend. Create new Express router `runbooks-editor.ts` exposing 7 endpoints for role-scoped runbook editing on managed hosts. Byte-shape mirror of Phase 44's `skills-editor.ts` (D-01, D-17), with three structural adaptations:
