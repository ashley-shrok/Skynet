<the_ios_pwa_trap>
⚠️ **PATTERNS flags a trap that would ship nothing on desktop.** The in-repo visibility-reconnect analogs in `PrettyView.tsx` and `Terminal.tsx` both OPEN with `if (!isIosPwa()) return;`. Copying that gate means the feature does not exist on Chrome desktop, Android, or non-PWA Safari.

That gate exists for a reason that does not apply here: `PrettyView`'s comment explains it guards a **session-attachment race** — on platforms where WebSockets survive tab switches, a force-reconnect creates a race against the existing attachment. **fleet-status is read-only fanout with no attachment semantics**, and D-12 wants wake-on-visible everywhere.

So: **take the guard SET from `PrettyView` but NOT the `isIosPwa()` gate.** The guards worth copying are the ones that make the handler idempotent under iOS PWA's spurious double-fires: check for an already-open socket before connecting, clear a pending timer on hidden without resetting the attempt count, and respect the disposed flag. Record the divergence in a comment so a future reader does not "restore" the gate.
</the_ios_pwa_trap>
