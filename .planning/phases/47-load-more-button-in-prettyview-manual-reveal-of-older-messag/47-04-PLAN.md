Post-revision architectural note: The cursor semantic revised from `beforeEventId: string` to `beforeLine: number` (Plan 01 change). This plan's client now tracks `oldestLoadedLine: number | null` alongside `messages[]`, seeded from the initial hydration frames' new `line?: number` field (min across hydration) and advanced to `parsed.oldestLine` on every successful fetch-older-range-batch response.
