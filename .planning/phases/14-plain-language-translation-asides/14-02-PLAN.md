<objective>
Compose Wave 1's three primitives (injectBtw, sendEscapeToBtw, extractBtwAnswer) plus the two constants (BTW_PROMPT, ASIDE_END_MARKER) plus the local shellQuote helper into the full backend aside subsystem — under the frontend-arm architecture locked in CONTEXT.md § Trigger (2026-07-26). This plan brings the backend to feature-complete for ASIDE-01, ASIDE-02, ASIDE-08, ASIDE-09, ASIDE-10, ASIDE-11 (frontend rendering + morph + arm-emitter land in 14-03 / 14-04).

Purpose: The Trigger architecture has one non-negotiable pivot from the pre-revision draft: **the arm signal is a WS message from the frontend, NOT a backend-observed idle event.** The terminal WSS (port 30002, in `terminal.ts`) and the pretty-view WSS (port 30011, in `claude-session-server.ts`) are separate closures on separate ports with no shared state — the frontend PrettyView already has `isIdle` in scope as a prop from Terminal.tsx (established Phase 9) and IS the natural single-source-of-truth for the arm signal. Frontend gates on `pvIdentity !== null` before emitting, so backend doesn't need identity awareness.

The other non-negotiable pivot: **per-connection aside state (`armed`, `displayed`) lives in a module-scope `Map<WebSocket, {armed, displayed}>`**, not as `let` variables inside `wss.on("connection")`. Cross-tab dismiss broadcast MUST flip peer WSes' Map entries in addition to sending them the dismiss frame — otherwise the peer's overlap-ignore gate stays stuck on `displayed: true` and silently breaks ASIDE-08 across tabs.

Output: A backward-compatible extension of the pretty-view WS event surface (two new backend→client events + two new client→backend payloads) plus the full arming/polling/broadcast machinery under the frontend-arm architecture and module-scope state model.
</objective>
