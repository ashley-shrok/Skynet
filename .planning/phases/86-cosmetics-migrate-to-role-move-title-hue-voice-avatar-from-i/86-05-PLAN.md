<objective>
Add the inherit-vs-override affordance layer to `IdentityModal.tsx` edit block per D-CTX-86-surface-5. Each cosmetic field (Title, Voice, ColorHue, Avatar) gains two visible states: (1) UNSET on identity → shows role's default with "Inherited" marker; (2) SET on identity → shows the value with a "revert to role default" affordance that, when clicked, deletes the field from the identity's frontmatter.

Purpose: Wave-2 frontend delivery of D-CTX-86-surface-5. Depends on Plan 86-01 (the `Identity.roleDefaults` field must exist on the type this modal consumes). Independent of Plans 86-02, 86-03, 86-04 (different file, different concern).

Output: `IdentityModal.tsx` edit block with per-field inherit/override affordances; new `handleRevert(field)` helper that sends `null` in the PUT payload; dirty tracking extended to detect revert-of-unmodified-field.
</objective>
