<objective>
Extend the fleet-status sweep with source-D: enumerate `~/fleet/interactive-messages/*/` on the agent's box and emit one JSONL line per widget carrying `line_kind:"interactive-message"`, plus widen the TS-side parser to route these lines into their own bucket. This unlocks port lookup + type-tagged discovery for widgets. Additive per Phase 118 precedent — SWEEP_SCHEMA_VERSION is NOT bumped.

Purpose: without discovery, the /interactive/ proxy has nowhere to look up widget ports. This is the foundation the backend router (Plan 03) and the registry adapter (Plan 02) both consume.
Output: sweep emits widget lines; parser accepts them; frontend/backend downstream can now type-dispatch on line kind.
</objective>
