1. **`matrix-message-fetch.ts` + `matrix-message-send.ts` services** — thin composition layers over Plan 03's `getRoomMessages` + `sendMessageAsUser` primitives, translating WS-level payloads (beforeEventId cursor, mqid-as-txnId) to the primitive-level API.
