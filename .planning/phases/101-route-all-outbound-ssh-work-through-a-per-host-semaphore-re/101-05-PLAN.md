<objective>
Wrap three session-managed producers. This is the trickiest cluster of the
phase because the shapes vary:

1. `server-stats.ts` fans out ~8 widget collectors on ONE Client — wrapping
   at the fanout entry point (`collectMetrics`) is byte-cheaper AND
   semantically correct: it holds ONE slot for the entire widget batch
   instead of trying to individually wrap the widget primitives (which live
   in `system-collector.ts` and have no hostId in scope — D-05 correction).

2. `file-manager.ts::openDedicatedTransferSession` is the resolver
   `host-transfer.ts` funnels through — wrapping it covers all 10 caller
   sites for free (D-06). `host-transfer.ts` stays untouched.

3. `file-manager-session.ts` has `execChannel` / `execWithSudoBuffer` that
   consume `SSHSession` objects with an optional `hostId?: number` field.
   Where hostId is present, we wrap. Where it isn't (legacy or migrated-
   session cases), we fall through unchanged — a small future-work TODO but
   not a blocker for Phase 101 since the CI grep will surface uncovered
   sites in Plan 101-06.

Output: Three files wrapped, tests green.

Parallel-safe with 101-03 and 101-04 (file-disjoint).
</objective>
