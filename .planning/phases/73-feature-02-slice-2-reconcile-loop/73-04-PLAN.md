<objective>
Wire the pure decision layer from Plan 03 to real SSH transport, then plug the composed sweep into ssh-poll-orchestrator.ts at the natural moment a per-host channel first becomes usable. Fire-and-forget from the poll's perspective — the sweep runs on the orchestrator's channel but its errors are contained: hangs, throws, restart failures all log and continue rather than propagating up. Once-per-host-per-instance-lifetime gating lives in an in-memory Set; container restart naturally re-fires the sweep on next acquisition. The opt-in `runs_fleet_substrate` flag from Plan 02 is the filter that lets an operator veto a host.

Purpose: This is where the shape doc's "bundled bytes reach hosts" actually happens. Everything upstream (catalog, migration, decision logic, log tags) has been prep; this plan lands the transport + hook + integration test.

Output: A running Skynet instance sweeps every opt-in identity-hosting host on first successful channel acquisition, pushes stale bytes over the existing channel, restarts agent-supervisor.service where needed, emits fleet_substrate_* log lines, and never degrades the 2s fleet-status poll.
