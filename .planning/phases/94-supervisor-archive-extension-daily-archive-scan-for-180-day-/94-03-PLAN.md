<objective>
Wire the archive scan into the supervisor's existing reconcile loop. Two functions are added: `run_archive_scan_if_due()` (the 24h cadence gate — cheap fast-path exit on most ticks) and `run_archive_scan()` (the actual per-identity scan that applies the guards from Wave 1 and dispatches to `retire_identity` from Wave 2). A single-line hook is added inside `reconcile()` between `sample_memory` (L1289) and `resolve_identities` (L1290) per RESEARCH § D-01. The retire-stuck counter (D-14) lands here — a per-identity plain-integer counter file in `$DORMANCY_STATE_DIR/`, incremented on each daily-pass retire failure, reset on success, dropping the `retire-stuck` sentinel + emitting a LOUD `ERROR:` log line when the count reaches 3.

Purpose: This is the plan where the archive-scan mechanism actually STARTS RUNNING on the fleet. Everything up to Wave 2 was dormant code. This plan flips the switch. Three decisions that RESEARCH § "Open Questions" flagged for planner discretion are resolved and encoded here:

- Q1 (MODE-agnostic scan): the loop walks `$IDENTITIES_DIR/*/` directly, NOT `IDENTITIES[@]`, so MODE=A boxes still sweep every local identity — because a box that owns an identity's folder is responsible for archiving it whether or not the supervisor is actively keeping it alive.
- Q2 (collision handling): resolved in Wave 2 (retire_identity aborts on active+archive collision so the counter drives to retire-stuck; no defensive epoch suffix).
- Q3 (retire-stuck counter): Option A — a plain-integer counter file at `$DORMANCY_STATE_DIR/retire-fail-count-<name>`. Reset on successful retire from inside `run_archive_scan` (not from inside `retire_identity` — the caller owns the counter). Sentinel is presence-only per the .pinned / .no-dormancy convention.

Also codified: Pitfall 5 lock (marker mtime updated unconditionally at end of scan — even when retires failed — because the gate is about cadence, not success). Pitfall 2 lock (skip literal 'archive' directory in the identity walk). And the inline-vs-background decision: inline (per RESEARCH's recommendation; the scan is bounded and the daily cadence means the burst happens at most once per 24h per box).

Output: One edit to `substrate/scripts/agent-supervisor.sh` — adds two functions (grouped with the archive-scan helpers landed in Waves 1 and 2), adds retire-stuck counter increment/reset/sentinel logic inside `run_archive_scan`, and adds a single line `run_archive_scan_if_due` inside `reconcile()`. This is the plan after which the mechanism is fully wired on-fleet.
</objective>
