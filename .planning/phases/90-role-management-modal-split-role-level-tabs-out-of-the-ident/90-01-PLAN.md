Extend the existing `GET /roles?hostId=<n>` route to include role-level cosmetic frontmatter (title, displayName, colorHue, voice, avatar) alongside the existing `{name, description}` shape. Directly enables D-05 (`.pv-row` rendering keyed on role's colorHue) and D-06 (role modal chrome hue continuity from row → modal).

Output: extended `roles-list-for-host.ts` handler + extended `RoleSummary` type on the frontend + companion test coverage.
</objective>
