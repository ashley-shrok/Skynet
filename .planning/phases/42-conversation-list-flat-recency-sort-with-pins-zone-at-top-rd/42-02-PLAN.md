<objective>
Add an always-in-DOM search input at the top of the conversation list, hidden by scroll on cold load, that flattens the three zones into a single label-only match list when the user types. The reveal-by-scroll-up is the same on every platform; no auto-focus on mobile.

Purpose: This plan ships the search affordance independently of both the sort/zone reshape (Plan 01) and the recency-signal wire-protocol extension (Plan 03). It plugs into the existing panel structure and reuses the established bounty-count-filter renderer pattern (PrettyConversationsPanel.tsx:525-532) and the established sessionStorage-sentinel pattern (conversation-store.ts:129-180 `pv-conv-active-set`).

Output: A search input that mounts at the top of `.pv-panel-scroll`, a one-shot cold-load scroll-hide effect gated by a sessionStorage sentinel, a label-only filter predicate applied to the union of every zone (respecting the hidden-rows exclusion), and updated panel tests covering all four behaviors.
</objective>
