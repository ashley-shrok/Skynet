| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-103-14 | Denial of service | Unbounded tunnel-cache growth | accept | Per D-16 no cache eviction now; per-target tunnel = 1 net.Server + 1 SSH channel from pool (channel returns to pool on tunnel close). | T-103-17 | Information disclosure | Interstitial HTML leaking error internals | mitigate | interstitial.ts renderInterstitial accepts NO err argument; only errorClass discriminator + typed ServeTarget fields. Info-leak invariant T-40-05 inherited from Phase 78 pretty-view pattern. |
</threat_model>
