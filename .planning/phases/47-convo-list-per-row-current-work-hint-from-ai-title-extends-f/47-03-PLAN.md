<objective>
Land the working-store third axis (aiTitle) with a last-wins reconciliation chokepoint, mirroring the Phase 44 Plan 03 max-wins chokepoint architecture for lastMessageAt. The store now consolidates aiTitle writes from BOTH feeds — the fleet-status WS publish path AND the /sessions/list seed path (AppShell wire consumer lands in Plan 47-04) — through a single last-wins chokepoint `advanceSessionAiTitle`.

Purpose: unblock Plan 47-04 (AppShell seed loop + PrettyConversationRow subtitle consumer). The chokepoint is where the "topic drifts across turns" semantics live — CONTEXT.md § Working-store third axis makes this explicit: "If WS says 'Debug X' and later WS says 'Fix Y', we want 'Fix Y'." Different from lastMessageAt's monotonically-increasing max-wins rule.

Output: `useSessionAiTitle(sessionKey): string | null` hook available for row-level subscription; three-axis co-change frame emits 3 notifies (one per axis) matching the single-chokepoint observable contract Phase 44 Plan 03 Test 15 established for two axes.
</objective>
