<objective>
The novel load-bearing infrastructure of this phase: a per-user always-on live-event pump that polls Matrix, filters new events through the classifier's `harness_dm` rule, and dispatches push notifications. Plus its boot-time starter. This is the trigger for every push notification the phase delivers — without this loop, the PWA opt-in works but no notifications ever fire.

Purpose:
- D-01 per-event shape check (classifyRoom on every new event, not stored designation)
- D-02 reuse harness_dm classifier concept (do NOT re-derive)
- D-03 per-event, not cached-per-pair (robustness over efficiency)
- D-04 only new agent-side messages (filter out self, edits, reactions, joins, leaves, system events)
- D-14 fire on every subscribed device (via sendPushToUser which handles multi-device)

The push-trigger-loop is the second per-user always-on loop in the codebase (the first is observation-loop.ts). Both patterns come together here: observation-loop's scheduler shape + relay-room-stream-server's fetchLive primitive.

Output: Two modules + colocated dep-injection tests + a starter ready for Plan 08 to invoke from starter.ts.
</objective>
