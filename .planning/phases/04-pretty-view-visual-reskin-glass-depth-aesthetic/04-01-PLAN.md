**Design rationale for the `lg` treatment:**

- Positioning `top-3 right-3` (slightly further from the corner than `md`'s `top-2 right-2`) — pretty view has more breathing room than a cramped terminal pane; the extra 4px reads as "considered placement" rather than "hugging the corner."
- `flex-row` + avatar-on-left + text-stacked-on-right: the mock's structural choice. Reads as a nameplate ("identity: Tina Wilson, Bounty Coordinator") rather than a corner sticker. The `md` treatment's vertical stack (avatar-on-top) is the right choice for a smaller footprint; the `lg` treatment's horizontal stack takes the space needed for name + title.
- `borderRadius: 12` (not `9999` full-pill) — the mock's `.pv-identity` treatment is a rounded rectangle, not a pill. Reads as a card, matches the ambient-panels-shelf treatment.
- `background: linear-gradient(160deg, rgba(60,50,35,0.75), rgba(34,28,20,0.8))` + `backdropFilter: blur(24px) saturate(1.4)`: warm-dark glass base per mock lines 409-418.
