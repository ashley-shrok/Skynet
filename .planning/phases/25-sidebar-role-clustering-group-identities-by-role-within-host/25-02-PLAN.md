<objective>
Wire `identity.role` onto every ConversationRow at construction time, add a new `(host, role, label)` comparator, and swap it in at the four sort call sites in `computeSnapshot`. This is the behavior change Ashley locked: same-role identities cluster within their host bucket in all three tiers (ActiveSet / Pinned / Tier-3), no visible chrome, null-role rows sort to the bottom.

Purpose: Clone-adjacency. When a role has multiple identities (clones share a role by construction — Phase 22 SRIC-03), their rows appear together in the list. Colorhue inheritance on clones already gives an implicit "same-hue adjacent" cue; this sort turns that cue into position guarantee.

Output:
- `ConversationRow` type carries an optional `role?: string | null` field.
- `state.identitiesByKey` module-level input (mirrors `state.hostsFlat` pattern from Plan 07-01) plus an `updateIdentitiesByKey(map)` mutation.
- `rowFromTab` and the `fleetSyntheticRows` construction site both resolve role via `state.identitiesByKey.get(sessionMatchKey(targetTmuxSession))?.role ?? null`.
- New `compareByHostRoleLabel` comparator replaces `compareByLabel` at four sites: `:365` (ActiveSet), `:403` (Pinned), `:431` (Tier-3 known-host bucket), `:458` (Tier-3 orphan-host bucket).
- `compareByLabel` at `:523` (rdpRows.sort) is ALSO switched to the new comparator for consistency (per PATTERNS.md §Whether to keep compareByLabel — safe because RDP rows have undefined role → null-role-last treatment is inert within the RDP sentinel bucket).
- AppShell pushes `useIdentities().byKey` into the store on identity-store change via a new `useEffect` mirroring the `updateHostsFlat` effect at `:500-502`.
</objective>
