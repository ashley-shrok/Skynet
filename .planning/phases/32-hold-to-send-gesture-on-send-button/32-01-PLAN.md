<objective>
Wave 1 of Phase 32: (a) make `useVoiceRecording.cancel()` race-safe against a pending `getUserMedia` (defensive fix — enables the hold-gesture short-tap rollback to work correctly on slow mic-permission grants), and (b) build the reusable `useHoldToRecord` hook that owns the press-and-hold gesture layer for the send button.

The hook is consumed by Plan 32-02 at BOTH send-button render sites (primary and queue slot) inside ComposeBox.tsx. Isolating the gesture logic in a hook (a) makes the primary/slot wiring symmetric, (b) allows the gesture behavior to be unit-tested in isolation from ComposeBox's ~2878-line surface, and (c) gives the D-16-02 iOS Safari sync-getUserMedia invariant a single audited chokepoint instead of duplicating it at two callsites.

**Implementation shape:** Shape 1 (optimistic start + rollback) from CONTEXT.md `<specifics>` L116 — pointerdown calls `voice.start()` synchronously (guarantees the iOS Safari gesture invariant); if pointerup fires before the 250ms threshold, the recording is rolled back via `await voice.cancel()` (awaited to keep the ordering deterministic) and the caller's short-tap callback fires. Shape 2 (setTimeout(250) before voice.start) is rejected because CONTEXT.md L64 requires an iOS Safari 26.6 prototype to validate it, and no such prototype exists in this working tree.

**Task ordering rationale:** The useVoiceRecording race fix goes FIRST (Task 1) because the useHoldToRecord hook (Task 2) depends on cancel() being race-safe — a short-tap that happens before getUserMedia resolves must reliably tear down the pending recording. Without the pendingCancelRef fix, cancel() is a no-op in that window and the mic would stay open indefinitely.

Purpose: single-audited-chokepoint for the iOS sync-gesture invariant; enables symmetric wiring at two ComposeBox render sites; hardens the underlying voice hook against a real race that pre-exists this phase (a short-tap on hold-send with a slow permission grant would otherwise leave the mic hot).

Output: `useVoiceRecording.ts` widened with pendingCancelRef, `useVoiceRecording.test.ts` widened with 3 tests, `useHoldToRecord.ts` + `useHoldToRecord.test.tsx` (both new files) — all tsc-clean, vitest-green.
