The state transition is orthogonal to whether the /id reset text renders as a message. Per revised CONTEXT.md § domain + § decisions (Migration strategy) + § test-strategy: detection is detect-and-emit, NOT detect-and-suppress.

Per PS30-02 + CONTEXT.md § Backend observations feeding pane_state → holding: the parser observation is the first-priority trigger; the existing Layer 1 tail-state reducer stays in place as a redundant safety-belt path (dedupe in the emitter ensures ONE wire frame). Layer 2 discovery-repoll stays for the SIGTERM-fallback case where the parser never sees /id reset.

The change is TWO-part: (a) parser gains a pure `detectIdReset(obj)` predicate (no side effects, no message-emission changes); (b) onLine dispatch in claude-session-server.ts calls the predicate on every raw line and fires `paneStateEmitter.emit("holding", "id_reset")` on true, WHILE the message emission runs to completion alongside.

Purpose: PS30-02 requires the parser observation as the earliest real signal, and the emitter from Plan 30-01 is the funnel this observation flows through. Preserving the message-emission path is required by Ashley's HARD LOCK (per revised CONTEXT.md).
