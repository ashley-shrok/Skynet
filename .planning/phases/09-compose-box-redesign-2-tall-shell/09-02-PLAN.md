<objective>
Rotate the segmented context meter well from vertical (28px wide × stretched-tall) to horizontal (160px wide × 28px tall), preserving every visual class value and animation timing from patch #83 except where the horizontal orientation demands a rewrite (flex direction, segment iteration order, drain-sweep delay direction, divider orientation). Purpose: Free up top-row real estate for future buttons (per Ashley 2026-07-22: "over time we're going to add more buttons on the top row… no need for it to be stretching across the whole way when it's just going to end up shrinking over time anyway") while preserving the segmented-well "one instrument" reading from patch #83.

</objective>
