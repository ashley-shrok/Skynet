# Campaign: a New skill option at the bottom of the composer's quick Skills menu

**Opened:** 2026-10-28
**Status:** in_progress
**Workspace:** ~/fleet/identities/cedar-box-maintainer-6/workspace/skynet/.planning

## Concept

When you scroll the composer's Skills menu and realize none of the skills there fit, you can make a new one right from that menu instead of going to the sidebar footer. A last row, New skill…, closes the menu and walks you through naming and describing the skill for this conversation's machine, then drops you in the new skill's editor.

## Success criteria

- The composer's Skills menu always ends with a New skill… row, including mid-search.
- Choosing it gets you to a freshly made skill on this conversation's machine without passing through the sidebar.
- Backing out at any point leaves you in the conversation, as if nothing happened.
